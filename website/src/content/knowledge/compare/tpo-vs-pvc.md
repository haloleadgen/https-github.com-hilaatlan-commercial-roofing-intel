## How this comparison was built

Fixed criteria applied identically, per the [CRI Methodology](/about/methodology/):
service life, seams, chemical exposure, fire, cost, track record, and best-fit conditions.
Sources are standards and technical data, not marketing.

## Side by side

| Criterion | TPO | PVC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical service life | 15–25 years | 20–30 years |
| Seams | Hot-air welded | Hot-air welded |
| Grease/chemical resistance | Limited | High |
| Field track record | ~30 years | 50+ years |
| Formulation consistency | Varies by manufacturer | More standardized |
| Typical cost position | Lower | Premium |
| Late-life repairability | Good | Harder (brittleness) |

## What actually decides it

- **Restaurant / food service / industrial exhaust:** PVC, full stop. Grease degrades
  TPO and can void its warranty.
- **Standard office, warehouse, retail with clean exposure:** TPO's price advantage
  usually holds; PVC's premium buys resistance the roof will never use.
- **Owner risk posture:** the longest welded-membrane history is PVC's. Owners who pay
  for track record are buying something real, if rarely decisive.
- **Cold-weather installation:** PVC generally welds more forgivingly in marginal
  temperatures — an installation-quality consideration, not a performance one.

## Common mistakes

1. Comparing a 45-mil TPO bid against a 60-mil PVC bid and calling PVC "expensive."
2. Putting TPO over a kitchen exhaust field to save the premium — then losing the
   warranty argument later.
3. Treating all TPO as identical; manufacturer formulation history matters more in TPO
   than in any other membrane. Ask for the specific product's field history.

## FAQ

**Is KEE membrane the same as PVC?**
KEE (Ketone Ethylene Ester) membranes are a related thermoplastic with plasticizer
stability advantages — worth pricing on harsh-exposure roofs; typically a further premium.

**Can PVC and TPO be repaired with each other's material?**
No — they are not weld-compatible. Repairs must match the membrane chemistry.
