## Plain-English definition

A membrane attachment method using rows of screws and plates driven through the
membrane edge into the roof deck. The next sheet overlaps and is sealed over the
fastener row, so the finished roof shows no exposed fasteners — they are hidden inside
the seams.

## Why it matters to you

**Verified:** Mechanical attachment is generally the fastest and most economical way to
secure a single-ply membrane, which is why it is so common on large roofs
([GAF — single-ply attachment methods](https://www.gaf.com/en-us/blog/building-science/a-practical-guide-to-single-ply-roofing-attachment-methods-bc56373c-9ef5-466d-9cd4-f4014a31d13d)).
The trade-off is that the membrane is only held at the fastener rows, so wind can pull
air into the assembly and make the sheet billow and flutter between rows — behavior
that [fully adhered](/glossary/fully-adhered/) and induction-welded systems reduce or
eliminate. For owners, the practical questions are whether the proposed fastener
pattern matches the calculated [wind uplift](/glossary/wind-uplift/) pressures for your
building's zones, and whether the fasteners are compatible with your deck type — a bid
that is cheaper because it thins out the fastening is not a bargain (see [adhered vs.
mechanically attached](/knowledge/compare/adhered-vs-mechanically-attached/)).

## Technical detail

Fastener row spacing and in-row spacing are engineering outputs, not installer
preference: manufacturer requirements and building codes set the pattern, and under
[ANSI/SPRI WD-1
(2025)](https://www.spri.org/download/ansi-spri_standards_2020_restructure/wd-1/ANSI-SPRI-WD-1-2025-Wind-Design-Standard-Procedure-for-Non-Ballasted-Roofing-Assemblies.pdf)
the tested uplift capacity of the assembly is compared against the design load for each
roof zone. Because design pressures rise from the field to the perimeter and corners,
fastening density typically tightens toward roof edges. Fastener pullout resistance
depends on the deck (steel, wood, concrete), which is why deck condition and type are
verified before this method is specified.
