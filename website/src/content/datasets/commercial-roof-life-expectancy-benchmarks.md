## Benchmark table (v0.1 — sample data)

| Roof system | Typical range (years) | Well-maintained upper bound | Key life-limiting factors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TPO (60 mil) | 15–25 | ~28 | Seam quality, UV, ponding |
| EPDM (60 mil) | 20–30 | ~35 | Shrinkage, seam adhesive aging |
| PVC (60 mil) | 20–30 | ~32 | Plasticizer loss, chemical exposure |
| Modified bitumen (2-ply) | 15–25 | ~30 | Surfacing wear, flashing failure |
| Built-up (4-ply, gravel) | 20–30 | ~40 | Membrane fatigue, drainage |
| Metal (standing seam) | 30–45 | ~60 | Fastener/seam corrosion, coating life |
