Glossary

Ponding water

Water that remains on a roof surface more than 48 hours after rainfall ends — a leading accelerant of membrane aging and a common warranty exclusion.

Plain-English definition

If water is still sitting on your roof two days after it stopped raining, that’s ponding water. Roofs are designed to shed water; where it collects instead, the membrane ages faster, debris accumulates, and structural load increases.

Why it matters to you

Verified: NRCA defines ponding as water remaining more than 48 hours after rainfall under drying conditions, and manufacturer warranties commonly require positive drainage or exclude damage from standing water (NRCA guidance, ARMA — Ponding Water Basics). Remediation typically means drainage correction: added drains, scuppers, or tapered insulation.
