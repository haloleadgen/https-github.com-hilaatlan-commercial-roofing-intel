Data & Research

Datasets, benchmarks, and research — each published with its methodology, coverage, and limitations alongside the numbers. Nothing here is citable until it has been validated, and we say so on the page rather than in the footnotes.

Citation policy

Nothing here is citable yet. CRI has published no validated dataset. What exists below is preliminary and is marked as such on the page itself — no suggested citation is offered, because offering one would invite exactly the reliance the data cannot bear. When a dataset reaches v1.0, it will carry a stable citation format and may be cited with attribution and a link.
