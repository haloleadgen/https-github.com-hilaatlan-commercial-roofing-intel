# Complimentary Commercial Roof Assessment 
Everything on CRI is planning-grade information. Decisions need building-specific facts. The assessment is how you get them — documented, no-obligation, and yours to keep. 
 
 
 

## What it is 

A structured on-roof evaluation by a vetted commercial roofing contractor:
 membrane and flashing condition, drainage, penetrations, visible moisture
 indicators, and photo documentation — summarized in a written report addressed
 to you. It's the same first step a consultant would bill for, offered
 complimentary through CRI's disclosed partner network.
 

## What you receive 
- A documented walk-through with date-stamped photos. 
- A written condition summary: what's sound, what needs attention, what to watch. 
- A useful artifact for budgets, reserve studies, insurance files, and warranty records — regardless of whether any work ever follows. 

## The ground rules (these are policy, not marketing) 
- No obligation. The report is yours either way. 
- No sales pressure. Partners agree to a no-pressure standard as a condition of receiving CRI referrals; pressure tactics are grounds for removal from the network. If you experience anything different, tell us. 
- Disclosed relationship. If you later choose to engage a partner for work, CRI may receive a referral fee. This is how the free platform is funded — documented on Funding & Relationships and How CRI Works. 
- Editorial independence is untouched. Nothing you read on CRI is written to sell assessments; the assessment exists for readers who ask for it. 

## When an assessment makes sense 
- Before a repair-or-replace decision — it's the condition data the framework needs. 
- When the Life Expectancy Estimator returns a short or low-confidence range. 
- Ahead of hurricane season, to create the pre-storm condition record. 
- At property purchase, lease renewal, or reserve-study time.
